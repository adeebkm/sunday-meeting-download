Sunday Meeting

A small floating window that listens to your meeting, shows a live transcript of both sides, and tells you what to say when you ask.

Adeeb built Sunday Meeting and made it free for students who need a hand in meetings, and to help them prepare for vivas and interviews.

Download for Mac

Version 0.1.0 · about 205 MB · needs a Mac with Apple Silicon and your own Claude API key

What it does

Hears both sidesYour microphone and the call audio are transcribed on your Mac, labelled You and Them.
What to sayPress the green arrow for a reply in your voice. Make it shorter, longer, or try a different example.
Ask anythingType a question about the conversation, ask follow-ups, or get a summary with action items.
ModesStarts with modes for meetings, interviews, sales calls and classes. Add your own notes and documents.
Private modeHide the window from screen sharing and recordings with one click.
Meeting historyPast meetings are saved with a transcript and summary you can search and ask about.

Install

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Sunday Meeting into Applications.
  2. Open it. This is an independent app that Apple has not reviewed, so macOS will block it the first time. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  3. Paste your Claude API key on the welcome screen. You can create one at console.anthropic.com.
  4. Press Start and allow Microphone and Screen & System Audio Recording when macOS asks. Quit and reopen the app once after allowing.

Your data stays on your Mac

Stored locallyYour API key, modes, documents and meeting history are saved only on your device.
Transcribed locallyAudio never leaves your Mac. Speech is turned into text on-device.
What is sentOnly transcript text and your mode notes go to the Claude API, using your own key, when you ask for something.
