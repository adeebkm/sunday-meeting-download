Sunday Meeting

A small floating window that listens to your meeting, shows a live transcript of both sides, and tells you what to say when you ask.

Adeeb built Sunday Meeting and made it free for students who need a hand in meetings, and to help them prepare for vivas and interviews.

What it does

Hears both sidesYour microphone and the call audio are transcribed on your Mac, labelled You and Them.
What to sayPress Say, or Control+Return from any app, for a reply in your voice on a large cue card that follows along as you speak.
Ask anythingType a question about the conversation, ask follow-ups, or get a summary with action items.
ModesStarts with modes for meetings, interviews, vivas, sales calls and classes. Add your own notes and documents.
Screenshot buttonPress the camera to send what is on your screen with the question, for slides, diagrams, code and problems.
Private modeHide the window from screen sharing and recordings with one click.
Meeting historyPast meetings are saved with a transcript and summary you can search and ask about.

Before you start

  • A Mac with Apple Silicon. Click the Apple menu, then About This Mac. If the Chip line says Apple M1, M2, M3 or later, it will work. Intel Macs are not supported.
  • A Claude API key with credits. Go to console.anthropic.com, sign in, add a few dollars of credit under Billing, then open API Keys and click Create Key. Copy the key; you will paste it into the app once.
  • Headphones, if you can. With speakers on, your microphone also hears the other person. The app filters that out, but headphones give the cleanest transcript.

Install, step by step

  1. Download. Click Download for Mac above. The file Sunday-Meeting-0.4.0-arm64.dmg lands in your Downloads folder.
  2. Enter the password. Double-click the downloaded file. macOS asks for a password: type the one Adeeb gave you.
  3. Copy it to Applications. A window opens showing Sunday Meeting and an Applications folder. Drag Sunday Meeting onto Applications. Then eject the Sunday Meeting disk in Finder.
  4. Open it once. Open your Applications folder and double-click Sunday Meeting. macOS shows "Sunday Meeting Not Opened". This is expected: it is an independent app that Apple has not reviewed. Click Done.
  5. Allow it. Open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security and scroll down to the Security section. Next to "Sunday Meeting was blocked", click Open Anyway, confirm with your password or Touch ID, then click Open Anyway again. You only do this once.
  6. Add your key. On the welcome screen, paste your Claude API key and save. It is stored only on your Mac.
  7. Find the window. Sunday Meeting is a small bar floating near the top of your screen. It has no Dock icon. Press Cmd + \ to hide or show it, and use Menu → Quit Sunday Meeting to quit. If anything is missing, a Setup checklist appears in the window with a button for each fix.
  8. Allow the microphone. Press Start. When macOS asks for the microphone, click Allow.
  9. Allow call audio. macOS then asks for Screen & System Audio Recording, which is how the app hears the other person. Click Open System Settings and switch Sunday Meeting on. Quit the app from Menu and open it again.
  10. Check both sides are heard. Press Start again. The two chips under the buttons should read You · mic and Them · call audio with a lit dot. If one is red, click it and follow the Setup checklist.

Using it

  1. Pick a mode from the dropdown: General meeting, Job interview, Viva, Sales or client call, or Lecture or class.
  2. Make it yours. Open Menu → Modes and settings, pick the mode, and replace the "About me (edit this)" line with your own details, or attach your resume, report or slides. Keep documents short; everything in a mode is sent with each request and uses your credits.
  3. Press Start when the meeting begins and End when it finishes. The meeting is saved to History on your Mac.
  4. Say (or Control + Return from any app): what to say right now. Camera (or Control + Shift + Return): the same, plus a screenshot of your screen. Cmd + Shift + \ switches to a compact window that shows only what to say. Type a question and press Return to ask anything about the conversation.

Updating

Download the new version, drag it to Applications and choose Replace. Your key, modes and history are kept. If the other person stops being heard after an update, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording, switch Sunday Meeting off and on again, then reopen the app.

Your data stays on your Mac

Stored locallyYour API key, modes, documents and meeting history are saved only on your device.
Transcribed locallyAudio never leaves your Mac. Speech is turned into text on-device.
What is sentOnly transcript text and your mode notes go to the Claude API, using your own key, when you ask for something.
